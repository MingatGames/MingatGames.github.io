About Studio

Playful, Accessible & Zero Friction

At Mingat Games, we believe great games shouldn’t require 50GB downloads, clunky app stores, or invasive telemetry. We build high-craft browser games that start playing immediately on click.

Whether it’s a tranquil 3D barista simulation with procedural jazz lofi chords, a gentle educational adventure empowering young learners, or a pastoral steppe survival experience, every Mingat game is crafted with passion for web performance, thoughtful accessibility, and charming aesthetics.
